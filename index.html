About BusSaathi

BusSaathi is a student project prototype designed to make college transport information easier to explore. Students can switch routes, inspect sample stops, view illustrative ETA information and use the help chatbot.

The current version is a demonstration. It does not connect to a real bus, transport office or live tracking database.
